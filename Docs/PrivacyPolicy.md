# DeviceMonitor Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

DeviceMonitor is built so that nothing about you or your network leaves your iPhone.

## What the app collects

Nothing. DeviceMonitor has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

## What stays on your device

To show your network, the app stores on your iPhone only:

- the devices it finds or you add: name, address, hostname, kind, open ports, advertised services and the notes, tags and nicknames you enter;
- their status history and latency samples (status changes for 90 days, latency for 7 days);
- your settings.

This data never leaves the device unless you export it yourself with the share sheet. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions

- **Local Network**: used to find devices on your Wi‑Fi and check whether they answer. The app only talks to devices on your local network; it does not contact servers on the internet.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access is requested.

## Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, the app can explain a device or your network in plain language with Apple's on-device model. The request contains device names, kinds, states and port numbers, is processed on your iPhone, and is not sent to Apple or anyone else.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the app's public repository. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/antonsmedberg/DeviceMonitor/issues>.
